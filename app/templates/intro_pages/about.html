About the Team

We’re a team of graduating IT students who came together to build BAG (Budgeting Aid Guide) as our capstone project. Our group combines strengths in back-end development, front-end design, database management, and visual design to create a complete and functional application. Each team member contributed in a meaningful way—from building core features and structuring the database to designing the user interface and shaping the overall look and feel of the platform. Throughout the project, we collaborated closely, adapted to challenges, and supported each other’s growth. BAG represents more than just a class assignment—it reflects our technical skills, teamwork, and dedication to building something useful for real students. As we graduate, we hope to continue improving and expanding the project beyond this course.

Our Goal

BAG was created with one goal in mind: to help students take control of their financial aid instead of feeling overwhelmed by it. Many students receive financial aid without a clear understanding of how to manage it across an entire semester. Our goal was to build a tool that simplifies that process—allowing users to track spending, stay organized, and make informed financial decisions. We designed BAG to feel approachable, supportive, and practical. By combining clean design with meaningful functionality, we aim to reduce stress, improve financial awareness, and help students make their resources last longer.
